## Finding an agent

Home lists the agents you can use. The agent directory groups them:

- **Your teams**: agents of the teams you belong to.
- **Across the organisation**: agents published to everyone who signs in.
- **Public**: public agents, while the platform allows public access.

<Screenshot slot="discover-agents" />

Search the directory by name or description: letters and digits are compared, so "wifi" finds "Wi-Fi" (v0.4.2). Your search shows as a chip beside the filters, and **Clear all** clears it with them. You can also type an agent's name in the command palette (⌘K or Ctrl+K). An agent may also have a public page or appear in a widget on a website; those work without signing in (see [Public pages and the widget](#public-pages-and-the-widget)).

## Asking a question

Open an agent and type your question, or pick one of its starter questions. Enter sends; Shift+Enter adds a line. A new chat starts with the cursor in the message box on a computer (not on a phone, where the keyboard would cover the welcome), and keyboard users reach it with **Skip to message box**, right after **Skip to content**.

- Until the first words arrive, the chat says what the agent is doing: "Understanding the question…" (a follow-up is being turned into a search), "Searching *agent*'s knowledge…", "Checking the passages…", "Thinking about your question…" (the model has the question), "Working on it…" (since v0.4.4: the answer is waiting for the model's connection to have capacity, for about a second or more; the step it interrupted comes back afterwards) and "Writing the answer…". Each step is shown only when it happens, and screen readers announce it once. This works the same, in the same words, on the chat page, in Try it, on public pages and in the widget (v0.4.2).
- The search shows as a step above the answer, for example "6 passages checked, 2 kept" when SystemOne judged them. A follow-up searched together with your earlier question reads "“…?” with your earlier question".
- The answer streams in as it's written. You never see a reasoning model's thinking, only "Thinking about your question…". Editors testing an agent in its **Try it** panel can open the reasoning; since v0.4.1 it's shown in the order things happened, with each part of the reasoning before or after the search or tool call it led to ([Try it](/docs/using/editors/agents#try-it)).
- After an answer with sources, up to three **follow-up questions** may appear under it a few seconds later, as chips: choose one to ask it. You don't have to wait for them; the answer is already complete. See [Follow-up suggestions](/docs/using/follow-up-suggestions).
- Agents know today's date in the time zone your platform admins chose ([Admin → Settings](/docs/administration/settings); UTC unless they changed it), so "today", "tomorrow" and "this week" mean your organisation's day. Before v0.4.1 the date was UTC's, so an evening question in the Americas could get tomorrow's date.
- An answer that was given recently to the same first question may arrive at once, all of it: it's a [saved answer](/docs/using/editors/saved-answers), with the same sources and checks as the original.
- Follow-up questions can refer to earlier turns ("and for a second copy?"). When a question depends on the conversation, Grounded rewrites it into a question that stands alone before it searches; a question that already stands alone is searched as you wrote it, which is faster. Since v0.4.2 a rewrite is used only when it reads as a search query: if the model answered instead of rewriting, the follow-up is searched with your earlier question, and the step never shows that text.
- With an agent that calls tools, the answer is the model's final turn. Narration it writes before a tool call ("I'll check the outage tool…") is taken back when the call starts and isn't kept (v0.4.2); the status goes back to "Thinking about your question…". Each call shows as a step named by the tool's title, such as "Used Check outage".
- **Stop** ends an answer while it's being written. The part you got is kept, a citation cut off midway ("[1") is dropped, and the answer says "Stopped. The answer may be incomplete." with **Ask again**, also after a reload (v0.4.2).
- If you're offline, the question stays in the box with **Retry**. An answer cut off midway says so and offers **Retry**.

### When the agent can't answer

Agents are strictly grounded unless their team turned that off. A strictly grounded agent answers only from its sources:

- If its sources cover part of your question, it answers that part and says what they don't cover.
- If they have nothing relevant, it replies with the team's refusal message and says it searched. The agent's starter questions appear below, as a hint of what it covers, also when you open the conversation again later (since v0.4.1).
- With a scope check on, a question outside the agent's subject gets "This is outside what *agent* covers." without a search.
- Greetings and small talk get a short reply and an offer to help.

An agent that isn't strictly grounded may also answer from the model's general knowledge, and must say which part isn't from the sources.

### Moderation

Public agents, and any other agent the platform or its team chooses, check questions and answers against a moderation policy. A blocked question or answer is replaced by a notice. How you see a checked answer depends on the policy ([Moderation modes](/docs/administration/moderation-modes)):

- **Paragraph by paragraph** (the default on public agents since v0.4.0): "Writing and checking the answer…", then the answer one paragraph at a time, each shown once it passed. If a later paragraph fails, the notice replaces the whole answer.
- **The whole answer**: "Writing and checking the answer…" until it's ready, then all of it at once, shown from its start.
- **As it's written**: the answer streams, and is replaced by the notice if it fails when it ends.

If the safety check itself is unavailable, a public agent refuses with "The safety check is unavailable right now. Please try again."

### Routers

Since v0.6.0, some agents are **routers**: front doors that answer nothing themselves and send your question to the agent that covers it, out of the agents you could chat with anyway. A routed answer says who gave it: **Answered by *Agent* · *Team***, with a **Continue there** link. It opens your own conversation with that agent, where you can ask a follow-up; a later question to the router that goes to the same agent continues that conversation. If the agent has been removed since, the line says "(removed since)" and has no link.

If no agent fits, the router says so in its own words. If it can't choose just now, it says "I can't send your question to the right assistant just now. Please try again later." The answer's citations and sources work as in any answer. A conversation with a router can't be shared; share your conversation with the agent it sent you to. Editors: see [Routers](/docs/using/editors/routers).

## Citations

Each factual sentence should end with a numbered citation chip such as **[1]**. Sources are numbered 1, 2, 3… in the order the answer first cites them, while it streams and after, so no number changes when the answer ends (v0.4.2). A chip stays on the same line as the word before it.

**Copy answer** (under the answer) copies the text with the same numbers, followed by its sources, each with its section ("Connect to campus Wi-Fi — Networks"). [Exports](#your-conversations) number the same way.

- Hover, click, or press Enter or Space on a chip to open its card, below the chip when there's room: the cited passage, with its heading path and pages. Focus moves into the card; Escape returns you to the chip.
- **Show source *n*** opens the cited passage in the **source viewer**, highlighted in its document beside the answer, with the claims that cite it ([Checking a source](/docs/using/source-viewer)). Where there's no viewer, such as a deleted agent's transcript, it reads **Show source *n* below** and opens the answer's sources, which start collapsed.
- The source cards under an answer open the viewer too.
- A web page is linked to its original URL when the agent's citation mode includes links. Uploaded files are cited by title only; you can't download them from a chat.
- Some agents can also call **tools** while they answer, such as a service status check. A tool's result is cited like a document and reads **From *Service status* · Check outage** among the sources: the service, then the tool's title (its name, such as `check_outage`, when it has no title; before v0.4.2 always the name). Claim checks treat it like any other source. A tool gets only the details the agent puts in a call, not your conversation. See [Tools from MCP servers](/docs/administration/mcp-servers#results-are-sources).

## Claims and verdicts

When the platform has a [SystemOne model](/docs/administration/systemone) and citation checks are on for the agent, Grounded checks each **claim**, a factual sentence of the answer (a list item or a table row counts as one), against the sources it cites. Each claim gets one verdict:

| Verdict | Meaning | On the chip |
|---|---|---|
| **Supported** | At least one source it cites supports it. | A check mark |
| **Not supported** | It cites sources, and none of them supports it. | A warning; red when its own source confidently contradicts it |
| **Uncited** | It states a fact but cites nothing. A small "Uncited" mark follows the sentence. | No chip |
| **Unchecked** | Nothing supports it and a check failed or timed out. | No mark |

Headings, questions, greetings and sentences such as "the sources don't mention the fee" aren't claims.

The answer has a one-line summary above its sources, such as "4 of 6 claims supported · 1 contradicted · 1 uncited". It counts supported claims over supported, not supported and uncited ones, and since v0.4.2 names every mark: "contradicted" for claims a cited source contradicts, "not supported" for the others; unchecked claims are left out.

A source is shown contradicting a claim only on a confident verdict (at or above the platform's auto-accept threshold, 80% by default). A less confident "contradicts" reads as not supported, in amber (v0.4.2).

A chip's card states its claim's verdict first, then what this source says about it, for example "Claim not supported. This source contradicts it (92% confidence)". A supporting verdict under 50% reads "with low confidence". Under the answer, each source card breaks down the claims citing it in the verdict colours: "Supports 3 claims · 1 not supported", or "Supports 2 claims · 1 contradicted" (before v0.4.0, "Supports 2 of 3 claims that cite it").

<Screenshot slot="chat-citation-card" />

- In a streamed answer, the checks run after the text is complete. "Checking the citations…" shows until they finish, then the verdicts appear in place.
- Some agents **enforce** the checks: citations that are confidently unsupported are removed, and if none of the answer's claims is supported, a strictly grounded agent replies with its refusal instead.
- Answers checked before v0.2.1 show a verdict per citation marker, without claims or a summary.

<Callout title="What a verdict means">
  A verdict says whether the cited passage backs the sentence. It isn't a judgment of whether the passage itself is correct. A supported claim is only as good as its source.
</Callout>

## Feedback

Each answer has thumbs up and thumbs down buttons; the one you chose stays pressed (no pop-up message; screen readers hear it). Since v0.4.2 you can take a rating back: press a pressed thumbs up again, or choose **Remove my rating** in the thumbs-down menu. A thumbs down asks for a reason from a fixed list: incorrect, not helpful, missing sources, wrong sources, outdated, harmful or unsafe, or other. The team sees counts of ratings and reasons in its analytics, never your question or the answer.

The thumbs-down menu also has **Share this question with the team**, off by default. Tick it before you choose a reason, and the team's editors can read your question (never who asked) in their [gap report](/docs/using/editors/gaps), to see what the agent is missing. Unticking it, or rating the answer up instead, takes the question back. Without the tick, a question the agent couldn't answer still counts towards the gap report's topics, but its text is never shown. A thumbs down also retires a [saved answer](/docs/using/editors/saved-answers), so the next person gets a fresh one.

<Screenshot slot="chat-feedback-share" />

If you're an editor on the agent's team, an answer you rated down, or one without sources, has **Add to evaluations**. It copies the question, and the documents the answer cited as the expected ones, into an evaluation set; nothing else from the conversation is copied. See [Evaluations](/docs/using/editors/evaluations).

## Your conversations

The sidebar lists your recent conversations. The **Conversations** page has all of them, searchable and filtered by agent and date. Times are relative ("2 days ago", counted by calendar day) with the exact time on hover, and a title cut short shows in full on hover. From a conversation's "…" menu you can:

- **Rename** it. Renaming doesn't change when it was last active.
- **Export** it as Markdown or JSON, with its citations. Since v0.4.2 the export numbers sources as the chat shows them, and the Markdown gives times in your time zone.
- **Share…** it as a read-only link ([Sharing a conversation](#sharing-a-conversation)).
- **Delete** it. It disappears for you at once. The stored copy is removed later according to your organisation's retention rules, which may keep it longer. Deleting a conversation also ends every link you made to it.

Nobody else can read your conversations unless you share one: not your team's admins, not platform admins. The other exception is a platform admin in a time-limited, audited break-glass session with the conversations scope; your team's owners are told when one starts and ends. Your organisation's terms of use may say more.

If an agent is deleted, its conversations stay readable for you, read-only. A link to a conversation that was deleted, or isn't yours, says "This conversation isn't available." with **Start a new chat**; a question typed there starts a new conversation.

The last answer's follow-up chips come back when you reload the page in the same browser tab (v0.4.2); another tab or browser shows the answer without them.

## Sharing a conversation

Since v0.6.0, **Share…** in a conversation's "…" menu (on the chat page and on **Conversations**) makes a link to a **read-only snapshot** of your own conversation: your questions, the answers and their citations, up to the last finished answer at that moment. It's your own deliberate act, and nothing else about who can read your conversations changes.

<Steps>
<Step>

### Choose who can open it

In **Share this conversation**, pick under **Who can open the link**:

- **People who can use *agent*** (the default): whoever the agent's audience lets chat with it, after they sign in. For a team agent that's the team's members; for an agent shared with everyone who signs in, anyone who is signed in.
- **Anyone with the link**: no sign-in. This is offered only for a **public** agent, and it's checked again every time the link is opened, so turning public access off, or making the agent less open, stops it working for people outside the agent's audience without touching the link.

Tick **Show my name** to put your display name (never your email) on the shared view. It's off by default.

</Step>
<Step>

### Create the link and copy it

Choose **Create link**. **Your link** is shown **once**: copy it now. Grounded keeps only a hash of it, so a later look at **Links you made** shows when it was made, how many messages it covers, who it was for and whether your name is shown, but never the link. To get a link again, make a new one.

</Step>
</Steps>

The dialog warns first: **anyone who opens the link can read this conversation**. You can't take back what someone has already read, so share it only with people you trust with what it says.

### What a viewer sees

The link opens a page outside the app, `/shared/<secret>`, that reads the same signed in or not: the conversation's title, "With *agent*", who shared it if you ticked **Show my name**, and the thread, with a notice that it's a read-only copy as it was when it was shared. Sources open beside the conversation, as in the chat, but only the passages the answers cited, never the whole document. **Ask *agent*** takes them to the agent. Someone the link isn't for, or who isn't signed in, is asked to **Sign in to open this conversation**; a link that never existed gets the same prompt, so nobody can tell valid links from invalid ones. Once signed in without access, they see "This conversation isn't available".

A viewer **never** sees your feedback, the model's reasoning, what the agent did to answer (its steps and searches), SystemOne's checks, or tokens and timings. A tool's result is named but its text isn't shown. A viewer sees no more than they would by asking the agent the same question.

### Revoking and when a link ends

In **Links you made**, the Revoke button (a bin icon) beside a link asks "Revoke this link?"; **Revoke link** stops it at once for everyone who has it. A link also ends when:

- you delete the conversation, or your organisation's retention removes it;
- the agent is disabled, unpublished or deleted;
- for **Anyone with the link**, public access is turned off or the agent stops being public;
- **you** stop being someone who can chat with the agent (since v0.6.1). A link works only while the person who made it is an active person who may still chat with the agent, and then for everybody, you included. If you leave the team, are suspended or lose access to the agent, the link stops for everyone, including links open to anyone. Nothing is deleted: if your standing comes back, so does the link.

### Limits to know

- A link covers the conversation **up to the last finished answer when you made it**. Later messages aren't in it; make a new link to share more. A stopped answer doesn't count, and a conversation with no finished answer can't be shared.
- At most 10 links per conversation. A conversation with a deleted agent, or one holding a [router's](#routers) answers, can't be shared.
- **Links don't expire** by time: they last until you revoke them, the conversation's own retention removes it, or one of the reasons above applies (a link that has stopped because its owner lost access isn't gone, and works again when that is put right). There are no view counts. A link is made and revoked, not edited.
- It needs you signed in; an API key can't make one.
- The platform's audit log records that a link was made or revoked and whether it was open to anyone, without the agent, the conversation or any content, and never in a team's log. For an agent at the Sensitive level or above, reading a shared answer is written to the [access log](/docs/administration/analytics-and-logs#access-log) like using the agent.

## Public pages and the widget

A public agent has a page of its own, and may be embedded on a website as a chat widget. Neither needs you to sign in.

- You chat in an anonymous session that ends 24 hours after its last use (the install may set another length). There's no history across sessions. Since v0.4.2 the session also ends when someone signs in or out in the same browser, so the next person on a shared computer doesn't see your conversation. **Sign in** on the page brings you back to the agent afterwards.
- **New chat** starts a new conversation within the session.
- Anonymous conversations are deleted after a short period, 24 hours by default.
- If you're signed in, a public agent's page keeps your conversations like any other agent's.
- Since v0.4.0 you can rate answers with the same thumbs, reasons and **Share this question with the team** tick as in the app, and open a citation's source in the [source viewer](/docs/using/source-viewer) (a full-screen sheet in the widget and on phones). Both work only for answers in your own session.
- The page's footer says that questions the assistant can't answer may be grouped, without your details, to improve it ([Gaps](/docs/using/editors/gaps#privacy)).
- Since v0.4.1 answers there offer [follow-up suggestions](/docs/using/follow-up-suggestions) too, when the agent has them on. On a phone, a long suggestion wraps inside its chip.
- The widget keeps a session of its own, separate from the public page's, even on the same site.
- In the widget, Escape closes the panel and returns focus to the launcher button. Since v0.4.2 the widget has one header, its starter questions in view, and the cursor in the message box when it opens.
- Both follow the light or dark setting of your device ([dark mode](/docs/using/personal-settings#public-pages-and-the-widget)).
