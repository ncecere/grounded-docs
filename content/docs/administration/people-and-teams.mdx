## Users

**People → Users** lists everyone who has signed in. Anyone your sign-in settings allow is created automatically on first sign-in, with no team; to build anything they need to be added to a team.

A user's page shows their profile, their teams and roles, and their activity. From it, platform admins can:

- **Change the platform role**: none, platform admin or platform auditor. Neither role grants access to team content.
- **Suspend** someone: they're signed out everywhere at once and can't sign in until reactivated.

The last active platform admin can't be demoted or suspended.

## Teams

Only platform admins create teams, though since v0.6.0 people can ask for one in the app and a platform admin approves it ([Team requests](#team-requests)). **People → Teams → Create team** asks for:

- **Name** and **Slug**: the slug is used in the team's addresses, such as `/teams/admissions`, and can't change later.
- **Description**.
- **Approved classification**: the most sensitive data the team may hold. Its sources and agents can use any level up to it.
- **Owner's email**: added now, or invited if they have never signed in.

A team's admin page has tabs for its **Overview**, **Members**, **SSO groups**, **Limits** and **Settings**:

- **Overview**: counts, usage against limits, recent changes, pending domain requests, and the **Budget** card (cost tracking and the monthly budget; see [Costs](/docs/administration/costs)).
- **Members**: the team's members. Owners manage their own members; a platform admin can **Assign an owner**, which promotes a member, adds someone who has signed in, or invites a new email address. An owner assigned this way is never changed by SSO group rules. Since v0.4.2 a team whose owner is still invited reads "Owner invited" (here, in the teams list and on its Overview), the tab lists open invites, and platform admins can revoke them.
- **Limits**: per-team overrides of the platform's limits. See [Limits](/docs/administration/limits).
- **SSO groups**: the rules that map identity-provider groups to roles in this team. See [SSO groups](/docs/administration/sso-groups).
- **Settings**: the name, description and approved classification, and archiving.

On your own user record, changing your platform role or status says "You'll lose access" before you confirm.

Platform admins don't see a team's sources, knowledge bases, agents' settings or conversations from here. A team's own pages show platform staff a notice instead of content.

### Archiving

**Archive this team** makes it read-only: members keep access but can't change anything. Rules for SSO groups that point at an archived team are ignored. You can unarchive it later.

## Team requests

People can't create teams themselves. There are two ways for them to ask:

- **In the app** (since v0.6.0, off by default). Turn on **Allow team requests** under Admin → Settings → Features, and people ask on the [Request a team](/docs/using/request-a-team) page. The **Requests** tab on **People → Teams** is the queue: approving a request creates the team. See [Team requests](/docs/administration/team-requests).
- **A form of your own.** Set `TEAM_REQUEST_URL` to your request form (a service-desk form, for example); Grounded links to it for people without a team while in-app requests are off. Without it, they're told to ask a platform admin, or pointed to `SUPPORT_URL` if it's set.
