---
title: Maintenance mode
description: Pause new ingestion across the platform while chat, search and reading keep working.
---

**Operations → Maintenance** pauses new ingestion for every team: uploads, syncs and crawls. Chat, search, reading and admin work carry on. Use it for risky changes, database work, or while a very large [profile migration](/docs/administration/embedding-profiles) competes for the embedding model.

## Turning it on

1. Turn on **Pause ingestion**.
2. Write a **Reason**. It's shown in a banner to people who manage content, and whenever an upload, sync or crawl is refused.
3. Optionally set a **Planned end**, to tell people when to expect ingestion back. Maintenance mode doesn't end by itself.
4. Confirm. New ingestion pauses on every server within a few seconds.

## While it's on

- New uploads, syncs and crawls are refused with `503 maintenance_mode`, the reason, and a `Retry-After` of the time to the planned end (or 5 minutes).
- Documents already queued are parked, not failed.
- Chat, retrieval, reading documents and conversations, and admin pages work as usual.
- Profile migrations keep running.
- The admin Overview's Features card shows maintenance as on. After 4 hours, the `GroundedMaintenanceModeLong` alert fires.

Refused uploads and crawls count against the availability objective, so keep maintenance windows short.

## Turning it off

Turn **Pause ingestion** off. Paused work continues when you save. Turning it on and off is audited.
